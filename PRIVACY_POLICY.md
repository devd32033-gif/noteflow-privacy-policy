# Privacy Policy for NoteFlow

Last Updated: December 6, 2025

## Introduction

Welcome to NoteFlow! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our note-taking application ("App"). By using NoteFlow, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
We may collect the following personally identifiable information:
- Email address (when you create an account)
- User-generated content (your notes and any images you add to notes)

### Non-Personal Information
We may collect non-personal information such as:
- Device information (device type, operating system)
- Usage statistics (how you interact with the app)

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve our services
- Enable synchronization of your notes across devices
- Authenticate your account
- Communicate with you regarding updates or support

## Data Storage and Security

Your notes are stored locally on your device and optionally synchronized with Firebase cloud services if you choose to enable cloud sync. We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data, but please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:
- With your consent
- To comply with legal obligations
- To protect our rights and property

## Your Rights

### Access and Update
You can access and update your information through the app settings.

### Data Deletion
You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time. When you delete your account:
- All your notes stored in the cloud will be permanently deleted
- Your account information will be removed from our systems
- Local copies of your notes on your device will remain until you uninstall the app

### Account Deletion Process
To delete your account:
1. Open the NoteFlow app
2. Navigate to Settings
3. Tap on "Delete Account"
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Please note that account deletion is irreversible and permanent.

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, you can do so directly from the app:
1. Open NoteFlow
